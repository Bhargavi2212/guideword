# Case K: second grader

## The answer key (these fields only)
- **root_cause**: The recipe was changed without any hazard review: a 12% larger batch with all the extra monomer put into the initial charge (and a changed solvent mix) raised the maximum reaction heat output to at least 2.3 times the standard recipe, beyond the capacity of the fouled reflux condenser, so the reaction ran away. Pressure rose and vapour leaked from the manway (closed with 4 of the 18 specified clamps, leaking at about 23 psig, far below the relief setting), forming a flammable vapour cloud.
- **component_ids**: R-1, E-1, R-1-MW
- **missed_by_original_review**: No process hazard analysis or reactive-hazard testing; recipe changes not controlled by management of change; condenser capacity undocumented and its fouling unmanaged; no automatic safeguards; improper manway clamping; no emergency evacuation plan.
