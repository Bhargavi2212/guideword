# Case Q: second grader

## The answer key (these fields only)
- **root_cause**: The humidified-nitrogen treatment hydrolysed the chlorosilane polymer deposits at low (uncontrolled winter) temperature, forming silanol/siloxane hydrolysis products that keep Si-Si bonds and are explosive and impact-sensitive; the following dry-nitrogen blows dried them, increasing their power and sensitivity. Impact or friction at the flange faces when the upper channel cover was opened ignited them. The hazard was unknown (no public literature), the procedure had no hydrolysis end-point or temperature criteria, and many bystanders stood around the exchanger.
- **component_ids**: HX-B
- **missed_by_original_review**: Risk assessment did not foresee an explosion of the deposits or their hydrolysis products; earlier events (burn injury, alkali explosion) not analysed deeply; work standard lacked objective criteria, hazard grading and review; no training on the hazard; non-workers allowed near the opening.
