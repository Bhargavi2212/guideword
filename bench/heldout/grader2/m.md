# Case M: second grader

## The answer key (these fields only)
- **root_cause**: The 6-inch carbon-steel de-ethaniser overhead line failed at the bend just downstream of the add-on wash-water injection point: the unquilled free jet of water stripped the protective iron sulphide layer and caused localised erosion-corrosion that thinned the wall from about 7-8 mm to 0.3 mm. The injection point and changes in its use (back to continuous) were never put through management of change, and it was missing from the inspection scheme and the RBI study, so the thinning was not found.
- **component_ids**: L-OVHD, WI-2
- **missed_by_original_review**: Injection point installed and its use changed without MOC; not in the written scheme of examination or the RBI study (believed isolated); downstream bend not inspected for about 20 years; corrosion monitoring and sampling lapsed; company bulletin on injection-point corrosion not applied to this point.
