# Case P: second grader

## The answer key (these fields only)
- **root_cause**: The idle column holding about 1,200 gal of MNT was 'isolated' from 300 psig steam only by closed single valves (manual block valves and non-tight control valves, no double block and bleed or blinds); corroded/eroded valves leaked steam into the reboilers when the boilers restarted, heating the bottoms to 415-454 F for about 8 days with nobody monitoring and no high-temperature alarm or heat-cutoff interlock, until the MNT underwent runaway thermal decomposition and the column ruptured. MNT thermal-stability hazards known from the batch still project were not applied to the continuous column.
- **component_ids**: C-1, SS-A, SS-B, E-1A, E-1B, FV-A, FV-B
- **missed_by_original_review**: No hazard evaluation of MNT thermal decomposition in the continuous column; no temperature alarm or heat-cutoff interlock; no positive isolation practice or monitoring for idle equipment containing MNT; steam valves not maintained tight; control room siting not evaluated.
