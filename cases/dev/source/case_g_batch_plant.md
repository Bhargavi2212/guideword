# Plant G: Silicone emulsions and blends, batch plant

## Plant data package

**Products**
- Silicone emulsions, release coatings and lubricant blends, made in batches of 2–8 tonnes.

**Raw materials kept on site (all arrive in 1,000-litre IBC totes or drums)**
- Polydimethylsiloxane fluids (several viscosities)
- Methylhydrogen siloxane fluid (contains Si–H groups)
- Vinyl-functional silicone polymer
- Platinum catalyst solution
- Non-ionic surfactants
- Emulsifier solution containing potassium hydroxide
- Acetic acid (for pH adjustment)
- Isopropyl alcohol
- Biocide
- Deionised water (from the plant system)
Totes are stored in a warehouse bay next to production. Some material is re-packaged into totes on site and labelled by hand.

**Equipment**
- Four stainless-steel mixing tanks (MX-1 to MX-4), 10 m³, with agitators, jacket heating/cooling, and loose lids with a manway.
- A portable air-operated diaphragm pump and hoses used to charge tank contents.
- A homogeniser and a filling line for drums and pails.

**Batch procedure (charging step)**
- The operator reads the paper batch sheet, collects the listed tote from the warehouse with a forklift, connects the hose and pumps the contents into the mixing tank, then signs the batch sheet.

**Building and safeguards**
- Single enclosed production building, ~2,000 m², general exhaust fans on the roof.
- Electrical area classification: unclassified, general-purpose equipment throughout.
- Smoke detectors and a sprinkler system.
- Jacket temperature control with a high-temperature alarm on each mixing tank.
- Personal protective equipment: gloves, goggles, chemical aprons.
