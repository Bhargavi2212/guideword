# Unit E: Fluid catalytic cracking (FCC) unit, planned shutdown (refinery)

## Unit data package

**Unit**
- Feed: heavy gas oil, ~25,000 bbl/day.
- Riser reactor: hot catalyst (~700 °C) contacts feed; cracked vapours go to the main fractionator.
- Regenerator: combustion air from the main air blower burns coke off the spent catalyst at ~700 °C, ~2 bar g.
- Catalyst circulates between reactor and regenerator through standpipes. Flow is controlled by a spent-catalyst slide valve (reactor → regenerator) and a regenerated-catalyst slide valve (regenerator → reactor).
- Slide valves: last overhauled at the turnaround 6 years ago.
- Main fractionator, then a wet gas compressor and a gas plant (absorber, stripper, debutaniser) handling C1–C4 gases and naphtha.
- Flue gas from the regenerator goes through an expander and a CO boiler to the stack.

**Nearby**
- Two asphalt storage tanks (~150 °C) 60 m from the reactor structure.
- An alkylation unit using hydrofluoric (HF) acid, 300 m away.
- Control room 120 m away.

**Planned shutdown procedure (summary of the written procedure)**
1. Reduce feed to minimum, then cut feed. Keep riser steam on.
2. Reduce main air blower rate. Keep the regenerator hot with torch oil if needed.
3. Withdraw catalyst from the regenerator to the spent catalyst hopper.
4. Close both slide valves as the catalyst inventory reaches minimum.
5. Steam-purge the reactor and the main fractionator to the flare.
6. Shut down the wet gas compressor; depressure the gas plant to the flare.
7. Shut down the main air blower; cool the regenerator.
8. Isolate and blind for maintenance entry.

**Safeguards**
- Basic process control system; slide valve differential-pressure indication and low-differential alarms.
- Reactor and regenerator temperature indication; flue gas oxygen analyser.
- Relief valves to the flare; emergency shutdown push buttons.
- Fixed fire water monitors.
