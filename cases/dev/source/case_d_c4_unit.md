# Unit D: Butadiene extraction unit (petrochemical plant)

## Unit data package

**Feed and products**
- Feed: crude C4 cut from an olefins plant (butanes, butenes, 1,3-butadiene ~45%, traces of acetylenes and water).
- Products: 1,3-butadiene (99.5%+) to two pressurised storage spheres; raffinate (butanes/butenes) to a pipeline; heavies to fuel.

**Major equipment**
- V-101 feed surge drum, 4 bar g, ambient temperature.
- C-101 / C-102 extractive distillation columns with solvent (N-methylpyrrolidone). Steam reboilers, cooling-water condensers.
- C-103 solvent stripper, operated at ~150 °C bottoms.
- C-104 butadiene rerun column, ~5 bar g. Reboiler E-104 (low-pressure steam), overhead condenser E-105, reflux drum V-104.
- P-104A/B product pumps (A runs, B is installed spare with suction and discharge block valves closed; switched over a few times a year).
- E-106 product cooler on the rundown line, with a normally closed bypass.
- Rundown line, 6" carbon steel, insulated, ~400 m to storage spheres TK-201/202.
- Flare header serving all relief valves.

**Line list notes (from the P&IDs)**
- 2" start-up recycle line from the P-104 discharge back to C-104, normally closed, used during start-ups (last used 14 months ago).
- 1" sample connections on the rundown line and at V-104.
- 3" bypass around E-106, normally closed.
- Spare-pump suction and discharge piping as above.
- 4" line to the off-spec tank, normally closed.

**Chemical injection**
- TBC (tert-butylcatechol) injected continuously at the V-104 outlet at 50–100 ppm.
- Antifoam injected to C-101.

**Instrumentation and safeguards**
- Basic process control system; high/low level alarms on all drums; high-pressure alarms on columns.
- Relief valves on all columns, drums and pump discharge to the flare.
- Hydrocarbon gas detectors at the pump area and the column base.
- Fire water deluge on the spheres.

**Inspection and maintenance**
- Wall-thickness checks by ultrasonic testing on the main process circuits every 5 years.
- Strainers upstream of P-104A/B, cleaned when the differential pressure is high.

**Site**
- Unit is about 300 m from the site boundary; residential area 1 km away.
