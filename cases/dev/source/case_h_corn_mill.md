# Plant H: Dry corn mill

## Plant data package

**Process**
- Receives shelled corn by truck and rail into storage silos.
- Cleaning, then tempering with water.
- Degermination, then roller mills and hammer mills, then rotary sifters, aspirators and purifiers.
- Products: grits, cornmeal and corn flour, conveyed to bins, then bagging and bulk loadout.

**Equipment**
- Bucket elevators between floors, screw conveyors and pneumatic conveying lines.
- Corn dryer (natural gas fired) for tempered product.
- Dust collection: ductwork from mills, sifters, conveyors and bagging, connected to shared baghouse collectors on the roof.
- Steam supply for the tempering area; compressed air system.
- 5-storey mill building; operators work on all floors; ~20 people per shift.

**Operating and maintenance records (last 3 years)**
- Bearing replacements on hammer mills and elevator head pulleys.
- 3 reports of smouldering material found in equipment, extinguished by the crew.
- Elevator belt misalignment events.
- Housekeeping crew on day shift.
- One lost-time injury from a fall on stairs.

**Safeguards**
- Basic motor control with interlocks on conveyor sequence start.
- Fire extinguishers and hose stations on each floor.
- Magnets upstream of the mills to remove metal.
