# Area F: Pulp mill evaporator and condensate area, two-week work window

## Area data package

**Process equipment in the area**
- Black liquor multiple-effect evaporators (steam heated), with a surface condenser and vacuum system.
- Three foul condensate tanks (T-31, T-32, T-33), each ~200 m³, atmospheric, fixed cone roof. Connected by a common overflow line and a common vent header that discharges to atmosphere above the roofline. Contents: condensate from the digester and evaporators (water with methanol, turpentine and other organic compounds; an organic layer can float on top). Levels vary with mill operation.
- A condensate stripper feed pump drawing from T-33.
- Weak black liquor storage tank (hot, caustic).
- White liquor line (hot sodium hydroxide / sodium sulfide) crossing the area on a pipe rack.
- Low-pressure steam header and cooling water lines.

**Planned work in the two-week window**
1. Contractor crew installs new pipe supports and replaces a corroded section of the 8" condensate line that runs across the roof of T-32 (cutting, welding and grinding; scaffold on the tank roof). Tanks stay in service during the work.
2. Mill maintenance replaces the mechanical seal on the stripper feed pump.
3. A 40-tonne mobile crane lifts the new pipe section over the white liquor line.
4. Insulation repair on the steam header (no hot work).

**Work control**
- Permit-to-work system with a hot work permit, a lockout/tagout procedure and a lifting plan procedure.
- Contractor safety orientation on day 1.
- Area operator signs off each permit.

**Safeguards**
- Eyewash and safety showers.
- Fire extinguishers at each work location; mill fire brigade.
- H2S personal monitors issued to mill operators.

## Task
Perform a full hazard review (HAZOP or equivalent) of this area for the two-week work window.
