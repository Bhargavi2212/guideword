# Plant R: wood pellet (granulation) unit at a sawmill site

## Data package

**Process / products**
- Wood-processing site of about 24 ha in a peri-urban business zone, operating under an environmental authorisation (wood storage, grinding of vegetable matter, woodworking, wood preservation). Softwood logs (pine, Douglas fir, spruce) are sawn into boards, which may be dried and/or treated. Intake about 550,000 m³/year of logs and billets.
- Sawmill outputs: planed wood, glued products, cross-laminated timber, pellet feedstock and bark (mulch, potting soil, internal fuel). Non-saleable billets go mainly to pellets.
- Pellet unit (in service about 13 years), average capacity 600 t/day: sawdust, shavings and chips (dried before grinding, possibly stored in silos) → grinding in hammer mills → pressing (starch is the only additive) → cooling → storage silos → dispatch. No hazardous chemicals are stored in the building.

**Major equipment**
- Pellet building, about 1,200 m², divided by a REI 120 wall into a cogeneration area (800 m²) and the granulation area (400 m²). Timber frame, steel-sheet roof, timber cladding (not fire-retardant). Three levels in two zones: grinding/refining zone and pressing/cooling zone.
- HM-1, HM-2 hammer mills (grinding zone), each with a stone trap with magnet at the inlet, inlet moisture monitoring, a vibration probe and a temperature probe (temperature probe not connected to supervision). Hammers inspected visually weekly. Each mill runs with excess air.
- Dust extraction, one chain per mill: mill outlet screw → duct → CF-1 / CF-2 cyclone bag filter → rotary airlock → ventilation duct → suction fan on the clean-air side. Each filter: differential pressure monitoring; bag cleaning on a programmed cycle every 4 h; three explosion vent panels of 0.5 m² each; automatic water extinction (solenoid valve and spray nozzle at the top) triggered by a glass bulb rated 80 °C.
- ATEX-certified items on the extraction: vents on each screw under a mill (4 per screw), suction fan, rotary airlock, pressure-relief stack, non-return valve on the extraction line.
- PR-1 to PR-6 pellet presses and coolers (pressing/cooling zone).
- Conveying: chain conveyors CC-1 and CC-2; screw conveyors; bucket elevators BE-1 and BE-2; a distributor. CC-2 and BE-2 are under the building roof. BE-1 has a decompression column with a fabric sleeve; the protective hood intended for it is not fitted.
- MC-1 main conveyor, about 400 m long, metal casing, carrying sawmill by-products; runs about 10 m from CF-1, in the axis of its vent panels.
- BC-1, BC-2 belt conveyors at a lower level, with plastic covers; rubber belts self-extinguishing to standard.
- SI-1 silo, galvanised steel, 750 m³ (currently about 30% full of dry material).
- SI-2 pellet silo, about 9,000 m³, about 30 m high (currently about 75% full of pellets).
- Cogeneration unit (800 m², behind the REI 120 wall).
- Site water basin serving both firefighting water supply and fire-water retention (volume not stated).

**Lines and connections**
- Mill outlet screw → suction duct → filter → airlock → ventilation duct → fan → atmosphere (clean side), normally running whenever the mill runs.
- Ground product from filter airlock and mill outlet to the pressing zone via screws, chain conveyors, elevators and distributor.
- MC-1 from the sawmill to the pellet unit.

**Chemicals / materials on site**
- Wood sawdust, shavings, chips and ground wood dust (combustible dust; lower explosive limit about 60 g/m³; self-ignition temperature of a 5 mm dust layer 310 °C per a third-party test report from 7 years ago).
- Wood pellets; starch; bark; logs and sawn timber; wood-preservation products (treatment area).

**Procedures**
1. Filter bag cleaning runs automatically on a 4-hour programmed cycle.
2. A supervision room on the upper level is permanently staffed; alarms are also called out to the operator's phone.
3. A permanent second-intervention team on site with fire lances and an internal water supply.
4. Periodic equipment checks under the site safety policy.
5. Two numbering systems are in use for the silos (one in the environmental permit file, one in internal supervision).

**Planned work / current operation** (this package describes a Sunday)
1. One line running: one hammer mill and three presses; 11 employees on site.
2. The running mill is temporarily running empty with suction on.
3. The supervision-room operator is helping a colleague in another building.
4. No hot work or contractor work planned.

**Instrumentation and safeguards**
- Spark detection and extinction: at the mill outlet screw, 2 detectors driving 4 nozzles in the screw and 1 at the screw end; on the suction duct before the filter, 1 detector driving 3 nozzles. Detector settings: hot particles at 400 °C, energy 20 mJ, air velocity 1–50 m/s; response 300 ms delay, 2 s spray, alarm to supervision. Thresholds elsewhere: presses 400 °C; coolers and output conveyor 250 °C.
- Filter explosion vent panels, water extinction (80 °C bulb), differential pressure monitoring.
- No sprinklers in the pellet unit.
- Site hazard study: 18 scenarios (14 with off-site effects); none concerns the pellet unit; no domino-effect study for the unit.

**Surroundings**
- Sawmill buildings connected by MC-1; log yard; drying and storage zone; wood treatment area; glued-products and cross-laminated timber production; pellet loading.
- Peri-urban business zone; nearest homes not stated.
