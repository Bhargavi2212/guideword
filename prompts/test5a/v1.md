## The checklist

Work through every step before you rank. Use only what the package states; where a step needs a fact the package does not give, say so in that hazard's reason instead of inventing it.

1. **Nodes.** Split the plant into sections: each vessel or piece of equipment with its lines, and each procedure or planned job.
2. **Process deviations.** For each section, apply the guidewords (no, more, less, reverse, as well as, part of, other than) to flow, pressure, temperature, level and composition. For each credible deviation, name the cause, the consequence if the safeguards fail, and the safeguards the package lists.
3. **Chemicals.** For every pair of chemicals that could meet (a shared line, vessel, drain, vent, hose or storage area), ask what happens if they mix. For each chemical, ask what happens with water, with air and with heat.
4. **Lines that can stop flowing.** For every line that can be isolated, blocked or left without flow, ask what happens to what stays in it (freezing, settling, reaction, corrosion, pressure build-up).
5. **Procedures.** For every procedure step, ask what happens if it is missed, done too much or too little, done in the wrong order, or done too early or too late.
6. **Planned work.** For every maintenance or construction job, ask what it does to the equipment it touches and to what is within reach of it: isolation, draining, opening, hot work, lifting, and what happens when it is returned to service.
7. **Dust.** For every piece of equipment that handles a combustible powder or dust, ask about dust explosions and fires, in the equipment and where dust collects outside it.
8. **Relief.** For every relief device, ask where its discharge goes and what it reaches.
9. **Rank.** Judge each hazard's severity (how bad, if the safeguards fail) and likelihood (how often the cause happens, given what the package says about the equipment and its history), and rank the hazards by risk, highest first. When two hazards are close, put first the one whose safeguards the package shows to be weaker.
