## The question

Do you recognise this plant, or the situation it describes, as a real accident or incident you know of?

- If you do, name what you can: the company, the site, the year and the accident as it is known. Name only what you actually recognise; leave out anything you would be guessing.
- If you do not, say so, and leave the names empty.
- Either way, give your reason: what in the package made you recognise it, or why nothing did.

Answer from what you know. Do not analyse the plant's hazards: that is not the question here.

## Answer format

Answer with one JSON object and nothing else, in exactly this shape. Use `null` for any name you do not give.

```json
{
  "recognised": false,
  "company": null,
  "site": null,
  "year": null,
  "accident_name": null,
  "reason": "<at most 300 characters: what you recognised and from what, or why nothing>"
}
```
